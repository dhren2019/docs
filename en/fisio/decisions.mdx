FISIO's **Análisis de la colocación** panel lists observations derived from the saved placement. These support the artist's review and are not autonomous approvals or diagnoses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save placement changes first">
    If you have changed the position, scale, rotation or projection mode, select **Guardar colocación** before recording decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each observation">
    Review the observed feature, its visual impact and whether it is labelled as observed or requiring context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a decision">
    Choose **Es intencionado** (intentional), **Revisar colocación** (review placement), or **No aplica** (not applicable). Consider a suggested adjustment if one is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your reasoning">
    Use **Tu criterio** to record why you intend to keep or revise the placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your review">
    Select a decision for each observation, then use **Guardar criterio FISIO**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the placement changes

Saved decisions belong to the placement and the observations available at the time. If those inputs change, FISIO can flag the prior review as outdated. Reconsider the observations before saving.

Completed or archived sessions are **read-only**.

<Note>Even an absence of observations does not validate the tattoo on the real client's body. FISIO displays a generic reference model.</Note>
