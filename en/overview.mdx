LIMINAL brings four tools into a tattoo artist's workflow: technical sheet generation, Color Engine, Piel Viva, and FISIO. This guide covers the features currently implemented in the beta and explains their boundaries.

<Note>Automatic observations are working references, not final professional decisions. Review the results against the actual session.</Note>

## How the tools connect

A **session** holds the client, design, body area, and work details. Generate a **technical sheet** from a reviewed session. Color Engine records colour planning decisions. Piel Viva organises photographic observations. FISIO helps you review the placement visually on a 3D model.

## Before you begin

- Select the correct session when switching between tools.
- Check any automatic interpretation before acting on it.
- Use client photography only with the appropriate permission and privacy safeguards.
