A practical way to begin with LIMINAL is to create a session from your design and review the resulting technical sheet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sessions">
    Start a new session from the Sessions area. The workflow begins with the design upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your design">
    Use a **JPG, PNG, or WebP** file no larger than **15 MB**. Wait for the image analysis to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the analysis">
    Check the proposed design details and correct anything that does not match your work. A **Ready** analysis still needs explicit artist confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the analysis">
    Select the design analysis confirmation action only when the review is complete. Saving corrections does not confirm the analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the session">
    Fill out the required client, body area, design, and technique information, then save the session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and review">
    From the session detail open **Generar ficha técnica**, start generation, and review the resulting sheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>A technical sheet requires a confirmed design analysis. If the analysis is still ready for review, return to the design step first.</Warning>
