Here are answers to common questions about the four LIMINAL beta tools.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is technical-sheet generation disabled?">
    Confirm the design analysis first. **Ready** still means pending artist confirmation. Return to the design screen, review its details and use the confirmation action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Color Engine show no candidate or mix?">
    Open **Mi tinta**, declare your actual stock and save it. Automatic mix suggestions need eligible documented references. Never replace missing evidence with an invented recipe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a HEX value as an ink recipe?">
    No. HEX is a display-colour sample. Any proposed MIX is an estimated visual starting point that requires testing and artist verification.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Piel Viva's comparison slider unavailable?">
    You need two different photos and confirmed manual alignment. Side-by-side viewing remains available before alignment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a limited photographic comparison mean?">
    Lighting, framing, image quality, positioning or insufficient overlap can prevent a trustworthy comparison. Review or retake the photographs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does FISIO not show a design on the model?">
    Check the session for a saved body model, valid placement and accessible design attachment. Outdated placement data must be reviewed in the session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not save FISIO decisions?">
    Save pending placement changes, choose a decision for every observation and ensure the session is editable. Closed and archived sessions are read-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can these tools diagnose a health condition?">
    No. Piel Viva documents visible photographic differences, and FISIO uses generic reference geometry. Neither is a medical diagnostic tool.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
