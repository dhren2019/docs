Color Engine's **Mezcla** section can offer an estimated starting point using eligible inks in your saved stock. The displayed amounts are not calibrated physical recipes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the real inventory">
    In **Mi tinta**, mark the bottles you actually own and save the inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a design target">
    Choose a colour target, check its screen sample and any execution context, and open **Resolver el objetivo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect an estimated mix">
    Expand **Mezcla**. Where supported, a candidate lists the component inks and whole-drop quantities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the starting point">
    Select **Usar como punto de partida** if appropriate. Report what you observe under **¿Qué observas en copa?**. In supported cases, the application may suggest a specific **+1 drop** change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm only your actual observation">
    When the real cup result is acceptable, select **Confirmar “está bien” en copa** and save the colour decision. Confirmation applies to this session, not all future sessions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Beta constraints

- Automatic MIX suggestions are limited to the supported Fusion visual references and compatible products explicitly marked as in stock.
- The solver approximates mixtures from product photographs. It has no calibrated pigment reflectance measurements.
- Suggestions generally favour two components; three are used only under the engine's decision rule.
- Dilution and greywash are separate from COLOR MIX and are not transformed into automatic recipes.
- If the evidence is inadequate, no reliable mix should be assumed.

<Warning>Estimated colour is not proof of physical ink equivalence or healed-skin appearance. Verify everything in your working conditions.</Warning>
