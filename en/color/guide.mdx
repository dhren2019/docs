Color Engine supports visual colour planning for an existing session. It can derive on-screen samples from the design, use an optional skin reference, and help you record choices based on the inks you actually have.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a session">
    Open Color Engine with a session containing a design. In **Diseño y objetivos**, inspect the image; a 3D view is also available when a saved placement exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your targets">
    Select **Reconocer colores** to propose samples from the image. Review each target's name, role, design area, and colour sample. You can sample a pixel directly or add a target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally add a skin reference">
    Upload a real reference photo, choose a representative area, and confirm it. Without an adequate reference, **Color Base** remains available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the execution target">
    Compare the original design sample with its skin-context execution target. Any manual override should include your reason. The original design colour remains recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve and save">
    Consider **Directa** (direct), **Mezcla** (mix), or **Comprar** (buy), according to the evidence available. Record your decision and use **Guardar decisión de color**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your ink collection

Use **Mi tinta** to declare which products are genuinely in your stock, set your region, and save preferences. Choosing a favourite brand does not automatically mark all its inks as available.

## Important distinctions

A **HEX** value is an on-screen RGB sample, not a physical pigment measurement. Camera-based skin context is an approximate visual reference, not calibrated colour science.

<Warning>Color Engine does not guarantee a physical ink match, healed colour, clinical result, or legal suitability of a product.</Warning>
