Piel Viva helps you document **visible differences** between real photos associated with one tattoo session. It is a photographic record and comparison tool, not a medical assessment.

## Timeline and checkpoints

The **Evolución** view supports the initial reference and session moments, together with follow-up checkpoints **D3, D7, D14, D30, and D90**. Missing photographs stay pending; LIMINAL does not fill them with simulated real results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the session">
    Open Piel Viva and select the correct session in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an initial skin reference">
    In **Referencia de piel**, upload a photo before the stencil when appropriate. Select and confirm a representative region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload checkpoint photographs">
    Under **Evolución**, choose the relevant checkpoint and select **Añadir foto**. Supported files are **JPG, PNG, or WebP**, up to **15 MB**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark an area">
    On a checkpoint with an image, select **Marcar zona** and choose the relevant area. Use **Zonas** to adjust its label, note, and boundaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your observations">
    In **Notas**, select the checkpoint, describe what you see and what you decide, and select **Guardar nota**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capture tips

Use consistent framing, soft light, a similar viewing angle, and unfiltered photographs whenever possible. If captures are too different, record the limitation rather than forcing a comparison.

<Warning>Piel Viva does not diagnose infection, illness, healing state, inflammation, hydration, or pigment retention percentages. Medical questions require a qualified healthcare professional.</Warning>
