The **Comparativa** tab in Piel Viva lets you compare two different photographs recorded for the same session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select two photographs">
    Choose **Foto A** and **Foto B** from the available checkpoints. The images must be different.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start side by side">
    **Lado a lado** displays both images. Use **Zoom conjunto** to inspect them at comparable magnification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align manually if needed">
    Switch to the overlaid alignment view and adjust the second photo's position, scale, and rotation against recognisable landmarks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm before using the slider">
    The comparison slider requires confirmed alignment. Without alignment, regional differences are not considered reliable comparable evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the comparison">
    Select **Guardar comparativa y observaciones**. Depending on image quality, the application can report that captures are comparable, limited, or should be repeated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Interpreting visual differences

Luminance, contrast, visible detail, and framing can change between photos for reasons unrelated to the skin. A visual difference does not establish a medical or physiological cause.

<Info>Automatic image registration is not part of the current beta. Alignment is manual, and comparisons can be labelled as requiring more context.</Info>

Use **Notas y criterio del artista** to preserve your interpretation. Return to the [Piel Viva guide](/en/piel-viva/guide) for photo and region setup.
