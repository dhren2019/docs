Once a technical sheet has been generated, LIMINAL provides an editor associated with its original session and a saved version history.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sheet">
    Use **Ver ficha técnica** from the session or from the completed generation workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the content">
    Read through the title and sections, cross-checking the output against the design, client details, and decisions made for this session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check freshness">
    A change to source inputs or a design can make a previous sheet outdated. If the interface warns that the sheet's origin is not verifiable, do not treat it as current.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect saved versions">
    The editor loads previous sheet versions. Use them to understand how the content has changed as the session evolves.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Professional responsibility

Generated text can organise your work, but it does not prove that every technical assumption is correct. Review and correct it using your own professional judgement.

<Tip>Whenever you change important source material, review the sheet's freshness indicator before reusing older content.</Tip>
