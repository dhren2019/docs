A LIMINAL technical sheet is generated from a saved session and a design analysis that the artist has explicitly reviewed and confirmed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the design">
    In Sessions, create a new session and upload a **JPG, PNG, or WebP** file of up to **15 MB**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the design analysis">
    Check the available suggestions, such as style, complexity, estimated time, estimated number of sessions, and colour palette. These values may be absent or require correction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct and confirm">
    Correct any inaccurate fields and then select **Confirmar análisis** (Confirm analysis). **Ready** is not the same as **Confirmed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the session information">
    Fill out the client and body area information together with design and technique details. Save the session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start generation">
    Open **Generar ficha técnica** (Generate technical sheet) from the session detail, review the available options, and select **Iniciar generación**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the technical sheet">
    When generation finishes, select **Ver ficha técnica** and review the content before using it in your professional work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before relying on the sheet

Check that the design file, body placement, client details, proposed duration, and technical assumptions match the real project. Generated information must not be treated as independently verified.

<Warning>The sheet is a structured work aid, not a medical instruction or a guarantee of technical outcome.</Warning>
